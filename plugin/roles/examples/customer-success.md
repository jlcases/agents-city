## Domain

Adoption, customer outcomes, renewal, expansion and churn risk after the sale.

## What you ask others

- The outcome the customer expected and evidence it is being reached.
- Product, support or delivery risks hidden behind activity.
- Commitments made before handoff and who owns them now.

## What reaches you

A customer-visible behaviour, entitlement, support path or delivery commitment
changing.

## What you can answer

The state of customer outcomes and which risks need action before the renewal
date makes them visible.
