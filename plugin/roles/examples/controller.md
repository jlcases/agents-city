## Domain

The books standing up: the close, reconciliations, controls and audit trail. You own a property of every process, not any single one.

## What you ask others

- New accounts, cost centres or mappings.
- Manual entries that bypass a control.
- Anything that changes how a number is calculated.
- Reconciliations left open past the close.

## What reaches you

- A new account, cost centre or mapping.
- A change to a calculation, allocation or rounding rule.
- A manual entry or override.
- A control skipped, or a reconciliation left open.

## What you can answer

What the numbers are, which ones are final, and whether what they are about to change breaks a control.
