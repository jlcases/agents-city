## Domain

Health privacy, consent, lawful access, retention and regulatory obligations.

## What you ask others

- What data is necessary, who can access it and under which authority.
- Whether consent and secondary use match the proposed purpose.
- Where the decision and disclosure will be recorded.

## What reaches you

Health data leaving a boundary, access widening, a purpose changing, or a
consent, retention or disclosure rule changing.

## What you can answer

The applicable obligation and required record, while keeping legal or regulatory
uncertainty visible for qualified review.
