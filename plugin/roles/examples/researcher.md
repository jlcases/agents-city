## Domain

The studies, sources or teaching materials you own and their full evidentiary
context.

## What you ask others

- Whether a method, source or construct is already available.
- Which assumptions a shared change invalidates.
- What evidence contradicts the emerging interpretation.

## What reaches you

A source, protocol, instrument or shared construct used by your work changing.

## What you can answer

What was observed, how it was obtained, what it does not establish and why a
choice in the study was deliberate.
