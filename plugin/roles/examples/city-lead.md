## Domain

Direction and accountable decisions in a domain the built-in catalogue does not
yet model.

## What you ask others

- What outcome is sought, what evidence matters and who has authority.
- Which assumptions or failure modes the city has not defined yet.
- What independent verification is proportionate.

## What reaches you

A decision changing the city's goal, authority boundary or accepted risk.

## What you can answer

Priority and final direction after the city has made its own evidence standard
explicit.
