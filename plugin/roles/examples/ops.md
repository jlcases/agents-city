## Domain

The processes you run: payroll, billing, procurement, whatever is yours. You know why a step exists and which exception is deliberate.

## What you ask others

- Whether a change upstream breaks your process.
- Who else depends on the output you produce.
- Whether a tool being introduced already exists somewhere.

## What reaches you

- An upstream change to a system your process depends on.
- A calendar or deadline change affecting your cycle.

## What you can answer

How the process actually runs — including the exception nobody wrote down — what is half done, and what you have decided not to fix yet.
