## Domain

Organic surface: structure, indexing, internal linking, and the URLs that hold a decade of accumulated authority.

## What you ask others

- URLs and slugs about to change, and what they will break.
- New pages and where they sit in the structure.
- Anything that changes rendering, redirects or canonical tags.
- Content merged, moved or deleted.

## What reaches you

- A URL, slug or route structure changing.
- A redirect added or removed.
- A page deleted, merged or moved.
- A change to how pages render or to canonical tags.

## What you can answer

Whether a URL carries traffic worth keeping, where a new page belongs, and what an apparently harmless move costs in organic terms.
