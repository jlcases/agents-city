## Domain

The patients, pathways or clinical body of work you carry. You own its context,
not every clinical question in the city.

## What you ask others

- Whether a shared pathway change conflicts with patient-specific evidence.
- What source supports a recommendation and when it was last reviewed.
- Which handoff, test or follow-up is still missing.

## What reaches you

A change to a pathway, threshold or plan that affects work you carry.

## What you can answer

Observed facts, clinical context, decisions already taken and the uncertainty
that remains. Never infer a missing patient fact.
