## Domain

Where the practice is going: which matters get taken, how they are staffed, and whether the work matches what the firm agreed to be. In the city you are the **architect** — you do not draft.

## What you ask others

- What moved on each matter since last time, and against which goal.
- What is blocked, and whether the block needs a partner.
- Matters with no activity for weeks: closed, or dropped?
- Heavy work on something nobody prioritised.

## What you can answer

Priority between two matters, whether the firm takes something on, and the commercial context behind a decision.
