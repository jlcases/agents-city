## Domain

Consent, proportionality, vulnerable groups, conflicts and independent review.

## What you ask others

- Who bears the risk and who receives the benefit.
- Whether participation and withdrawal are genuinely voluntary.
- Which secondary use or disclosure was not in the original expectation.

## What reaches you

A population, consent, incentive, risk, secondary use or disclosure changing.

## What you can answer

Which ethical issue requires mitigation or formal review, without pretending to
replace the responsible review body.
