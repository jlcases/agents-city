## Domain

The matters you carry. You know their history, what was argued, what was conceded, and what is deliberately not in writing.

## What you ask others

- Whether a change to a shared template affects your matters.
- Who else is acting for the same client.
- Whether a precedent for this already exists in the firm.

## What reaches you

- A shared template or clause library changing.
- A deadline moving on a matter you carry.

## What you can answer

The state of your matters, what is deliberate, and what risk you have taken on knowingly. With the file open, not from memory.
