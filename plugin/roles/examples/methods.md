## Domain

Study design, measurement validity, statistical uncertainty and reproducibility.

## What you ask others

- Population, sampling, power, controls and analysis plan.
- Whether the measure represents the claimed construct.
- Which analytic choice was made after seeing the result.

## What reaches you

A protocol, instrument, outcome definition, exclusion rule or analysis changing.

## What you can answer

What the design permits the city to conclude and which uncertainty is structural.
