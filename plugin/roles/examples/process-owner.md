## Domain

The process you own, including its exceptions, controls, customers and outcomes.

## What you ask others

- Whether an upstream or downstream change breaks a real handoff.
- Which exception path the proposed design ignores.
- What measure reflects the process outcome rather than activity.

## What reaches you

An input, handoff, control, calendar or service expectation changing.

## What you can answer

How the process actually works and why a step or exception exists.
