## Domain

The machinery that talks to customers on its own: sequences, segments, triggers, and the data they run on.

## What you ask others

- New automations, and what triggers them.
- Changes to a segment that other sequences also use.
- Anything that changes an event a sequence listens for.
- Sending volume and frequency caps.

## What reaches you

- A new automation or sequence.
- A change to a shared segment or to a trigger event.
- A change to sending frequency or volume.

## What you can answer

What is currently going out to whom, what a segment actually contains, and whether a new sequence overlaps one that already runs.
