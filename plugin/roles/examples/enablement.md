## Domain

Reusable commercial knowledge: playbooks, proof, onboarding and what the field
needs to make sound commitments.

## What you ask others

- What is being explained differently by different people.
- Which claim lacks maintained evidence.
- What successful account knowledge can be reused safely.

## What reaches you

A positioning, playbook, proof point or approved customer claim changing.

## What you can answer

What the field can responsibly say today and where knowledge is stale or missing.
