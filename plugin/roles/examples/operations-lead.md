## Domain

Operating direction, service outcomes, resource trade-offs and accountability.

## What you ask others

- Which accepted outcome moved, not merely which activity happened.
- Dependencies and exceptions that need an accountable decision.
- What should stop so the priority can actually be delivered.

## What reaches you

A material service, priority, resource or accepted-risk decision changing.

## What you can answer

Priority, ownership and operating trade-offs across programmes and processes.
