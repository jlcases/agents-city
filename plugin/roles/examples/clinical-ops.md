## Domain

Clinical capacity, pathways, handoffs, scheduling and operational continuity.

## What you ask others

- Which role owns every handoff and what happens outside normal hours.
- Capacity, queue, staffing and dependency assumptions.
- Whether the proposed flow works for the exception, not just the happy path.

## What reaches you

A pathway, handoff, staffing rule, service calendar or capacity assumption
changing.

## What you can answer

How care actually moves through the service and where an apparently small change
creates an operational gap.
