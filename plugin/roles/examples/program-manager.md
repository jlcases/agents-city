## Domain

Programme scope, dependencies, milestones, risks and acceptance across workstreams.

## What you ask others

- What is blocked, by which dependency and with what consequence.
- Whether a milestone represents accepted output or internal motion.
- Which scope change happened without a decision.

## What reaches you

A dependency, milestone, scope boundary, owner or delivery risk changing.

## What you can answer

The integrated delivery picture and where local progress does not produce the
programme outcome.
