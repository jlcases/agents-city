## Domain

Preventable harm, incident learning, safeguards and quality signals across every
care pathway.

## What you ask others

- What can fail, how it will be detected and who responds.
- Whether a near miss or incident has happened before.
- Which independent check protects the highest-consequence step.

## What reaches you

A safeguard removed, an escalation threshold changed, an incident, near miss or
new failure mode.

## What you can answer

Known failure patterns, current controls and whether verification is independent
enough for the risk.
