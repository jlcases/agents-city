## Domain

Where the brand is going and what it stands for. In the city you are the **architect**: you draw the plan and decide which sites go up. You do not write the copy.

## What you ask others

- What shipped since last time, and against which goal.
- What is stuck, and whether the block is yours to clear.
- Work that matches no goal — usually a missing goal, not drift.
- Anything going out under the brand that nobody reviewed.

## What you can answer

Priority between two campaigns, whether something fits the brand, and the business context behind a brief.
