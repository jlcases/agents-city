## Domain

Paid acquisition standing up: spend, bids, creative fatigue, landing speed and tracking. In the city you are the **surveyor** — you own a property of every campaign, not any single one.

## What you ask others

- New campaigns or budget changes, and what the cap is.
- New landing pages, and whether they load and convert.
- Creatives running long past their fatigue point.
- Anything that changes the conversion event.

## What reaches you

- A new campaign, or a budget change over your threshold.
- A new or changed landing page.
- A change to a conversion event or its value.
- A tracking parameter added, removed or renamed.

## What you can answer

What each channel is spending and returning, what is saturated, and whether the thing they are about to launch collides with something live.
