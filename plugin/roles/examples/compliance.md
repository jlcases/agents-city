## Domain

Obligations and deadlines: filings, retention, conflicts, and the record that proves what happened. In the city you are the **land surveyor** — you leave the marks, and **a mark that gets erased does not come back**.

## What you ask others

- New deadlines and who owns them.
- Anything touching retention, consent or personal data.
- New client relationships that could create a conflict.
- Changes to how the record of a decision is kept.

## What reaches you

- A deadline created, moved or missed.
- Any change to retention, consent or data handling.
- A new engagement that touches an existing client.
- A record or audit trail changing shape.

## What you can answer

What the obligation actually is, when it falls due, and what the record shows — which is the only thing that counts later.
