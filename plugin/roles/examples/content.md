## Domain

The pieces you own: articles, scripts, newsletters, landing copy. You know why a piece is written the way it is, and what was cut on purpose.

## What you ask others

- Whether a change to a shared template breaks your pieces.
- Who else publishes in the channel you are about to change.
- Whether the thing you are about to write already exists.

## What reaches you

- A shared template or component changing.
- A change to the publishing schedule of a channel you own.

## What you can answer

What you published and why, what is half written, and what you decided not to cover. With the piece in front of you, not from memory.
