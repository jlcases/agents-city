## Domain

The opportunities and accounts you carry: buyer context, objections, decisions
and commitments made.

## What you ask others

- Whether a proposed promise is approved and deliverable.
- Which customer evidence is current and directly observed.
- Who owns the next dependency outside the account.

## What reaches you

A product, price, delivery or policy change affecting one of your opportunities.

## What you can answer

What the customer actually said, what remains inferred and why the opportunity is
in its current state.
