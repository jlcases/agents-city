## Domain

What the firm knows and reuses: precedents, templates, and the work that got done twice because nobody knew.

## What you ask others

- What is being drafted from scratch that already exists.
- Templates diverging across teams.
- Work produced and never reused.
- What it would take for something that worked once to work again.

## What reaches you

- A new template or precedent created.
- Something being drafted that already exists elsewhere.

## What you can answer

What already exists in the firm before somebody drafts it again, and who to talk to.
