## Domain

Clinical measures, coding, provenance, data quality and comparability. What was
not captured at the point of care may be impossible to reconstruct later.

## What you ask others

- The source, population, time window and definition behind every measure.
- Whether a coding or collection change breaks a series.
- Which missingness is meaningful rather than random.

## What reaches you

A clinical measure, code set, source, collection rule or retention policy
changing.

## What you can answer

What the data actually supports, where it is incomplete and whether two numbers
are genuinely comparable.
