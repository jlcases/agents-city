## Domain

Research questions, portfolio direction, evidence standards and the decisions a
finding is meant to inform.

## What you ask others

- What decision the study changes and what evidence would change the conclusion.
- Whether the question duplicates existing work.
- Which uncertainty matters enough to investigate next.

## What reaches you

A research question, portfolio priority, conclusion or intended use changing.

## What you can answer

Priority and synthesis while preserving methodological dissent and uncertainty.
