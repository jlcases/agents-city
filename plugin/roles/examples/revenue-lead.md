## Domain

Commercial direction, revenue quality, forecast judgement and commitments across
the customer lifecycle.

## What you ask others

- Which customer evidence supports the forecast and which part is judgement.
- Commitments that product, delivery or finance has not accepted.
- Where pipeline movement does not correspond to buyer progress.

## What reaches you

A material forecast change, non-standard commitment, pricing exception or risk
to a strategic account.

## What you can answer

Priority, commercial trade-offs and whether the evidence justifies the call.
