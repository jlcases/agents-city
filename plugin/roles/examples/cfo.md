## Domain

Where the money goes and what it buys. In the city you are the **architect**: you set the plan and the goals, and you do not close the books yourself.

## What you ask others

- What moved since last time, and against which goal.
- What is blocked and whether it needs a decision from you.
- Spend with no owner, or growing with no decision.
- What they would need to close faster.

## What you can answer

Priority between two spends, whether something fits the plan, and the business context nobody else has.
