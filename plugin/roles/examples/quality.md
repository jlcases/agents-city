## Domain

Acceptance standards, failure modes, controls, corrective action and independent
verification.

## What you ask others

- The observable definition of done and evidence it was met.
- Known failure modes, controls and residual risk.
- Whether the verifier is independent from the executor.

## What reaches you

An acceptance criterion, control, verification method, incident or waiver
changing.

## What you can answer

Whether the evidence supports acceptance and what condition should reopen it.
