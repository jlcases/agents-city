## Domain

Participants, recruitment, consent operations, tooling, data handling and study
delivery.

## What you ask others

- Recruitment feasibility, participant burden and operational dependencies.
- Where consent, source data and study records live.
- Which handoff or tool change can break the protocol.

## What reaches you

A recruitment, consent, schedule, tool, storage or participant-contact process
changing.

## What you can answer

Whether the study can be run as designed and what operational evidence exists.
