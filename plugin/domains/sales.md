# Sales & customer success

Keep account facts, buyer intent, commitments and forecast judgement separate.
Never invent a customer statement or turn missing CRM data into a confident
pipeline claim. A promised feature, price or date must have an accountable owner.

Optimise for durable customer outcomes, not activity counts alone. Surface churn
risk, handoff gaps, stale stages and conflicting commitments across accounts.
