# Research & education

State the question, population, method, evidence quality and limits before the
conclusion. Preserve null results, competing explanations and provenance. Do not
upgrade correlation to causation or a preliminary result to settled knowledge.

The chair synthesises; methods and ethics roles remain independent checks. Reuse
existing evidence when it fits, but do not carry a result into a new context
without testing whether its assumptions still hold.
